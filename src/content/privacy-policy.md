# PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** 3 November 2026



## 1. Introduction

McRelish Ltd ("McRelish", "we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy of its customers, website visitors, and other individuals whose personal information we process.

We are committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you:

- Visit our website.
- Create or use a McRelish customer profile.
- Place an order.
- Contact us through WhatsApp, telephone, social media, or other channels.
- Purchase meals from our food hubs.
- Use our catering, food box, or other services.

This policy applies to our website and relevant business operations, including our existing and future food hubs.

## 3. How We Collect Personal Information

1. Create a McRelish profile.
2. Place an order through our website.

## 4. How We Use Your Personal Information

We process personal information for legitimate and specified purposes, including:

1. Creating and managing customer profiles.
2. Receiving and processing orders.
3. Preparing and fulfilling food orders.
4. Coordinating pickups and deliveries.
5. Confirming payments and maintaining transaction records.
6. Responding to customer enquiries and complaints.
7. Managing customer relationships.
8. Maintaining relevant order histories.
9. Improving our meals, products, services, and customer experience.
10. Managing our food hubs and centralised business operations.

13. Communicating important service information.
14. Sending promotional communications where we have an appropriate lawful basis and have obtained consent where required.

## 5. Legal Basis for Processing

McRelish processes personal information under applicable lawful bases provided by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Depending on the circumstances, these may include:

**Contractual necessity:** Processing information required to create an account, receive an order, process a transaction, or provide a requested service.

**Legal obligations:** Processing information required to meet applicable tax, accounting, regulatory, or other legal obligations.

**Consent:** Processing information where an individual has freely given consent for a specified purpose.

**Legitimate interests:** Processing information where McRelish has a legitimate business interest that is not overridden by the rights and freedoms of the individual.

Where consent is the applicable legal basis, individuals may withdraw it at any time.

## 6. Customer Profiles and Account Management

Customers may create a McRelish profile to access available account features and manage their interactions with our services.

Account information may be used to:

- Identify and authenticate customers.
- Manage account preferences.
- Facilitate orders.
- Display relevant order history.
- Provide customer support.
- Maintain account security.

Customers are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential.

Where an account is suspected to have been compromised, customers should notify McRelish through an official communication channel.

McRelish will apply appropriate access controls to protect customer account information.

Customers should only have access to their own private account information, subject to applicable law.

Creating a customer profile does not automatically subscribe an individual to marketing communications.

## 7. Marketing Communications and Customer Permissions

McRelish may communicate information about:

- New meals and products.
- Special offers and discounts.
- Promotional campaigns.
- Food boxes and family orders.
- Catering services.
- Special events and business announcements.

Where consent is required, we will obtain it separately from the information required to create an account or complete an order.

Customers may be given the option to receive promotional communications through supported channels, including WhatsApp, SMS, or email.

Customers may withdraw their marketing consent at any time using the available preference management or unsubscribe mechanism, or by contacting McRelish.

Withdrawing marketing consent does not automatically affect the processing of information required for order fulfilment, accounting, security, or other lawful purposes.

Essential transactional communications, such as order confirmations, payment updates, and pickup notifications, may continue where necessary.

We will not treat the provision of a telephone number, an existing customer relationship, or the placement of an order as automatic consent to promotional communications.

## 8. Sharing of Personal Information

McRelish does not sell customers' personal information.

We may share relevant information with authorised third parties where necessary to provide our services or fulfil legal obligations.

These may include:

- Website hosting and database providers.
- Payment processing providers.
- Delivery and logistics partners.
- Communication and messaging service providers.
- Technology and security service providers.
- Professional advisers.
- Government agencies and regulatory authorities where legally required.

We will take appropriate steps to ensure that third parties handling personal information on our behalf are subject to suitable confidentiality, security, and data protection obligations.

We will limit the information shared to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

## 9. Centralised Customer Database and Multiple Food Hubs

McRelish may operate multiple food hubs connected to a centralised customer and order management system.

This arrangement allows us to coordinate orders, manage customer profiles, maintain consistent service standards, and support business operations across our locations.

Personal information may be made available to authorised personnel where necessary to perform their responsibilities.

Access should be restricted according to job functions and operational requirements.

Personnel at individual food hubs should not automatically receive unrestricted access to the entire customer database.

## 10. Data Storage and Security

McRelish takes appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, destruction, and other forms of improper processing.

Depending on the systems and services deployed, these measures may include:

- Access restrictions and role-based permissions.
- Authentication and account security controls.
- Database security policies.
- Secure management of credentials and system secrets.
- Appropriate monitoring and logging.
- Restricted administrative access.
- Secure handling of customer records.
- Backup and recovery arrangements.
- Staff confidentiality and access management procedures.

Access to customer information should be limited to authorised personnel who require it to perform their duties.

Although we take reasonable steps to protect personal information, no electronic transmission or storage system can be represented as completely secure.

We continually review our safeguards as our business and technology develop.

## 11. Data Retention

McRelish retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal, accounting, tax, regulatory, and dispute resolution requirements.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

Where personal information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to securely delete or anonymise it, where legally permitted and technically feasible.

Requests for account closure or data deletion will be considered in accordance with applicable legal obligations and any lawful reasons for continued retention.

4. Request restriction of certain processing activities.

## 15. Personal Data Breaches

McRelish takes personal data security seriously.

Where we become aware of a suspected personal data breach, we will take appropriate steps to:

- Investigate the incident.
- Identify the information and systems affected.
- Contain the incident and reduce further exposure.
- Assess the potential risks to affected individuals.
- Take appropriate corrective measures.
- Document the incident and response.

Where a breach is likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of affected individuals, we will also communicate the breach to those individuals as required by law.
